Understanding and controlling biology at the molecular level

Yichao Zhao

I am Yichao Zhao, a postdoctoral researcher working with Professors Daniel Anderson and Robert Langer at MIT. My research focuses on developing biochemical electronics for continuous molecular sensing and precise control of cellular functions.

My current work focuses on electrochemical materials that translate electrical inputs into chemical cues to regulate specific cellular signaling pathways. This platform enables on-demand release of therapeutic proteins, providing a foundation for responsive cell-based therapies. I received my Ph.D. in Materials Science and Engineering from UCLA, advised by Professor Sam Emaminejad. There, I developed tissue-like biosensors for continuous molecular monitoring, enabling pharmacokinetic studies in animal models and metabolic monitoring in human participants.
